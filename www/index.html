Compound & Protocol Reference

HPLC Purity Standards (>98%)

A legitimate RP-HPLC chromatogram displays a single sharp peak. Ghost peaks or broad baseline shoulders indicate degradation, cleavage fragments, or synthesis contaminants.

Mass Spectrometry (ESI-MS / MALDI)

Mass spectrometry confirms molecular identity. Observed mass must correspond to the theoretical molecular weight within ±1 Da tolerance.
